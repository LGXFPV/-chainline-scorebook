# Chainline Scorebook

A disc golf scorekeeper that installs on your phone's home screen and works offline.

- 9 or 18 baskets, up to 100 players, cards (groups) for tournaments
- Handicaps, PDGA ratings and divisions, gross and net leaderboards
- Course log with par and distance for every hole
- A share card image at the end of each round

**Open it:** https://lgxfpv.github.io/-chainline-scorebook/

On iPhone, open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. On Android, open it in Chrome and choose **Install app** from the menu.

Scores are stored on each phone only. Use **Save backup file** on the Rounds tab to keep a copy or move to a new phone.
